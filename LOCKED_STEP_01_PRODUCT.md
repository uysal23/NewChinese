# LOCKED STEP 01 — Product
Android Simplified Chinese learning app covering HSK1–HSK6, 50 scenes per level, 300 scenes total. Each scene has two speakers, natural Mandarin dialogue, 2.5D/3D CGI visual, pinyin toggle, Turkish subtitle toggle, vocabulary/sentence/shadowing study, two-stage exam, favorites, free study, placement test, progress, reminders, themes and user name. Placement test: 30 questions, 5 from each HSK level. Scene unlock requires vocabulary exam >=90% and sentence exam >=85%. Character voices are persistent and natural; no phone TTS for main learning audio. App should load and build quickly and remain suitable for later Play Store commercialization.
